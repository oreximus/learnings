# Multiculturism - what I'm understanding:

1. A society where multiple cultures coexists.
2. A political arguments that says multiples cultures deserves respect and recognition.
3. State measures such as minority-language rights, religious exemptions, affirmative action, or cultural self-government.

- Multiculturism is essentially meaning that you are considering the rights and importance of the different cultures within the society and understanding the needs of saving their culture, like language, religion practices and other. Also for that state will have some policies and affirmative action to protect their community culture.

- This makes them free in terms of practicing their cultural activities and keeping their cultural lifestyle.

- But meanwhile we are considering this, this is also that providing the right to this cultural group can do the injustice to the vulnerable individual within it.

- Also considering the Will Kymlicka's version of multiculturism derives that providing such rights to groups can lead the problems like having the greater advantage of the majority culture - which for example he has given like "official language status" from the language of the majority culture. Likewise things related to the majority culture can create problems. So, there is a point to consider the fair chance to the minorities, immigrants, indigenous people, etc.
